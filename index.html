04 / Selected matters

Work from

A short selection of court matters in which Raghav Tiwari is recorded as appearing or holding brief. Summaries are limited to the available orders.

Criminal · Bail↗

Jugraj Yadav v. State of Chhattisgarh

Regular bail application under the NDPS Act concerning alleged possession of ganja. The High Court granted bail after considering the quantity, antecedents and stage of proceedings.

MCRC 8743/2024 · 16 DEC 2024 · CHHATTISGARH HC
Service · Recruitment↗

Vinay Kumar Nanda v. State of Chhattisgarh & Ors.

Writ challenge concerning a Chief Municipal Officer recruitment select list and objections to model answers. Petition dismissed after the Court considered the expert review process.

WPS 488/2014 · 27 AUG 2024 · CHHATTISGARH HC
Service · Regularisation↗

Jeevmangal Singh Tandan v. Union of India & Ors.

Service writ seeking consideration for regularisation and related employment reliefs in connection with contractual service at NIT Raipur. Petition dismissed.

WPS 1853/2014 · 31 JUL 2024 · CHHATTISGARH HC

Contempt proceedings relating to alleged non-compliance with an earlier writ order. The Court directed notice and called for an explanation; the displayed order sheet does not record a final determination.
